New
- Voice notes, like WhatsApp: hold the mic to record, slide left to cancel, slide up to lock and talk hands-free, then Send or Trash
- While recording: a timer, a live waveform and a short vibration when it starts
- The mic shows when the text box is empty and turns into Send when you type
- Voice notes go out as MMS (small AAC files, about 2.5 KB per second, so they fit in an MMS)
- Voice note bubbles: play/pause, a waveform you can tap or drag to jump, the length, and 1x / 1.5x / 2x speed
- A blue dot on voice notes you haven't played yet, and the sender's photo on incoming ones
- Only one voice note plays at a time
- Voice notes from other phones and apps play too (amr, 3gp, m4a, ogg)
- The + panel Voice button starts a locked (hands-free) recording
- Chat list and notifications show "🎤 Voice message" for voice notes

Fixed
- The mic asks for microphone permission the first time you use it
- Voice notes and the recording bar work in Yiddish/Hebrew (right-to-left) and in dark mode

New
- Forward like Google Messages: long-press a message → Forward opens the "Forward message" screen with search, "New message" and your recent conversations, each with its picture
- Search there filters as you type, by name or number; typing a number offers "Send to (845) …"
- Tap a chat and it opens with the forwarded message already in the typing bar (text, photos, videos, voice notes, files, and every attachment of an MMS), ready to send
- After you send (or press Back), you're right back in the chat you forwarded from, at the same spot, with a small "Forwarded to …" at the bottom
- Forward several messages at once: select them, then tap Forward at the top
- Send to several chats at once: in the Forward screen, hold a chat to pick more, then tap Send (each one gets it separately)

Fixed
- Forwarding a photo (or voice note, video) now works. Before, it only opened the share sheet with the text, so photos forwarded nothing
- Forward from the full-screen photo no longer throws you out to the chat list; you come back to the same chat
- Backing out of a forward without sending doesn't leave the forwarded text behind as a draft

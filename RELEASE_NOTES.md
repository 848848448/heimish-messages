New
- Videos, links, & more is now a full page with tabs: Photos, Videos, Links, and Files (when the chat has any). Swipe or tap between them
- Photos and videos show in a grid by month; tap a photo to open it, tap a video to play it. Videos show a preview frame and their length
- Links show the site name, the full link and the date; tap to open, long-press to copy or share
- Files (contact cards, PDFs, audio and more) open in the right app; long-press any photo, video or file to share it
- Details > Notifications page for each chat: show notifications on/off, Priority / Default / Silent, notification sound, vibrate, bubble, and "More settings" that opens Android's own page for this chat
- Snooze chat: 1 hour, 8 hours, 24 hours or 1 week; the row shows until when, tap again to wake it
- Bubble this conversation (Android 11+): turn chat bubbles on or off per chat
- SIM for this conversation on dual-SIM phones: pick which SIM this chat sends from
- Pin / Unpin, Mute, Archive / Unarchive and Delete conversation right in Details, with confirm dialogs
- Block & report spam: blocks the number for the whole phone, with "Report as spam" and "Delete conversation" options. Unblock shows when the number is already blocked
- Add contact / View contact button; long-press the number to copy it
- Groups: give the group a name and a picture, add people, tap a person to message, call, view or add contact, copy number, remove from group or block
- 1-on-1 chats: "Create group with ..." adds people and starts a group

Fixed
- Details now matches Google Messages section by section, with the same tinted cards
- Yiddish names in Details and the people list keep the right direction; phone numbers stay left-to-right
- The per-chat notification sound now lives in a real per-conversation notification channel, so Android's settings and the app agree

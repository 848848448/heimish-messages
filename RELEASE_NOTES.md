New
- Everything now looks and works like Google Messages
- Chat list: tap the round picture at the top for the new menu: Archived, Trash, Spam & blocked, Starred, Scheduled, Mark all as read, Settings
- Trash: deleted chats go to Trash for 30 days, you can restore them or delete them forever
- Search: new search screen with filters (Unread, Known, Unknown, Starred, Images, Videos, Places, Links) and results grouped in Conversations, Messages and Contacts
- + panel like Google Messages: big round buttons for Gallery, GIFs, Stickers, Files, Location, Contacts, Schedule send, Camera, Video, Voice
- Emoji panel with tabs (Emoji, GIFs, Stickers), search, recent emoji and categories; GIFs from your phone; tap a sticker to send it big
- Gallery right inside the chat: Camera, Folders and your recent photos, pick up to 10 with numbers, then Add
- Chat theme for every chat: pick a color and a wallpaper (gradient or your own photo), Apply, or reset to default. Open it from the chat menu or Details > Change colors
- New Details page: big picture and name, round Call / Video / Contact info / Search buttons, your photos, Change colors, Notifications, Block & report spam, and the people in the chat
- New Message details page
- New photo viewer: blurred background, sender and time on top, save, delete and share, and reactions on the bottom
- New chat screen: "New chat" with the To field, a big Create group button and contacts grouped by letter
